\section{Per-change measurements}
\label{sec:app-levers}

Table~\ref{tab:levers} gives the changes of \S\ref{sec:perf-changes} one at a time, on the measured configuration and workload of \S\ref{sec:perf-setup}.

Replacing the 30-bit sign decomposition, eight offset bits and seven chord-witness columns of the \texttt{Global} chip with three byte lookups removed 42 columns from 48\,M rows; the compressed proof kept its size while the core proofs, some $10^{8}$ bytes per block, shrank by 3.4\%. Removing redundant byte checks removed 5.7\% of the \LogUp{} pairs but only 1.4\% of time, because the lookup circuit's fold layers scale with padded heights rather than pairs. Narrowing a 20\,M-row chip by 11 columns was below noise. Raising the shard size by a quarter removed 16 shards (12\%) and 3.3\,s, about 0.2\,s per shard; a quarter-larger shard would predict 26 fewer, but some shards close on a chip's height budget rather than on the shard size; at 1.5 times the baseline size the 4.5\,GiB first-layer \LogUp{} buffer caused trim-and-retry and a 30\% slowdown, and twice the baseline did not fit in 32\,GB.
